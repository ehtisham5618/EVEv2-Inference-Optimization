=============================================================================
                          EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS
=============================================================================
Project: Profiling-Guided Inference Optimization for EVEv2 Vision-Language Model
Model:   BAAI/EVE-7B-HD-v2.0
Dataset: POPE (Polling-based Object Probing Evaluation) - 9,000 questions
Hardware: 2x NVIDIA Tesla T4 (15 GB each) via Kaggle
=============================================================================

1. OVERVIEW OF EXPERIMENTS
The objective of this experiment was to evaluate the EVEv2-7B-HD encoder-free Vision-Language Model on the POPE benchmark under constrained hardware limits (15 GB VRAM). Due to memory constraints, the model was distributed across two GPUs using FP16 precision. The model's hallucination rate and accuracy were tested across three dataset splits: Random, Popular, and Adversarial.

2. ACCURACY RESULTS (POPE Benchmark)
The model was evaluated using a binary (Yes/No) answer format across 9,000 test cases (3,000 per split).

4. COMPARISON AGAINST OFFICIAL BASELINE
- EVEv2 Official Paper Reported F1: 87.60%
- Our Constrained Reproduction F1:  83.78%
- Delta (Gap):                     -3.82%

Discussion of Delta:
The 3.82% accuracy gap compared to the official paper results is directly attributed to the severe hardware constraints of the evaluation environment. The Tesla T4 GPUs (15 GB) necessitated model offloading and FP16 half-precision casting. The PyTorch logs explicitly warned: "Some parameters are on the meta device because they were offloaded to the disk." This disk-offloading and lower precision caused minor degradation in inference quality, though the model still demonstrated strong hallucination resistance (above 81% accuracy) across all difficulty splits.
